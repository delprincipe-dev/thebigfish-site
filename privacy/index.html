Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

BigFish AI ("we," "our," or "the app") is built around a simple principle: your fishing data belongs to you. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it's handled, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

The following data is created and stored locally on your iPhone and is not uploaded to servers we operate:

  • Catch log — species, weight, length, notes, photos, and dates of fish you record
  • Locations — GPS coordinates and place names attached to your catches and spot scans, used to build your private hotzone map
  • Tackle box — lures you photograph and save
  • Profile answers — onboarding preferences (skill level, target species, fishing style) used to personalize the AI

Deleting the app deletes this data. We cannot access it.

Information processed by AI services

BigFish AI's intelligent features (catch identification, spot analysis, lure recognition, and the AI guide) are powered by OpenAI. When you use these features, the following is transmitted to OpenAI for processing and returning a result:

  • Photos you submit for analysis (fish, water, lures, or images sent in the AI guide chat)
  • Your questions and messages to the AI guide
  • Context that improves answers: your general profile preferences, recent catch summaries, tackle box contents, approximate location region (e.g., "Hudson River, New York"), and current weather conditions

This data is used solely to generate your result and is subject to OpenAI's API data usage policies, under which API data is not used to train models.

Weather and location services

  • Location — used with your permission to tag catches, find nearby fishing spots, and fetch local weather. Location is never shared with other users.
  • Weather — approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (a weather service) to retrieve conditions. No identifying information accompanies these requests.
  • Maps — nearby fishing spots are found using Apple Maps, subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up — we don't collect your name, email, or contacts
  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no analytics SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties
  • Your fishing spots are never shown to other users

Children

BigFish AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
